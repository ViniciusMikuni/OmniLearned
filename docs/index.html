01 / The foundation

OmniLearned Model

OmniLearned is a Point Cloud Foundation Model for Jet Physics. It builds upon OmniLearn with improved architecture and is pre-trained on 1 billion jets, with all datasets accessible from the software package!

01

OmniCosmos / Cosmology

Across research fields.
With less than half the data.

OmniCosmos adapts OmniLearned to galaxy and dark-matter halo point clouds, improving cosmological parameter inference with far fewer simulations.

100 / 600training examples for the σ8 comparison

With just 100 CAMELS-SAM training examples, the central R² for σ8 exceeds the published reference benchmarks shown at 600 examples. For Ωm, the improvement over those benchmarks appears at 300 examples.

These comparisons are to the reference algorithms in the figure, not to every model trained on the full dataset. Error bars show bootstrap uncertainty.

02

OmniMol / Molecular dynamics

Precise interatomic potentials.
Faster molecular inference.

OmniMol adapts the particle-physics foundation to molecular energies and forces, creating machine-learned interatomic potentials for small molecules.

~3× fasterOmniMol-m inference in the reported benchmark

On one A100 GPU, OmniMol-m is about three times faster than eSEN-md-d and AllScAIP-md for systems of roughly 100 atoms, while outperforming eSEN-md-d in energy error.

A speed–accuracy trade-off: AllScAIP-md has lower energy error. The result is specific to the models and benchmark reported in the paper.

03

MINERvA / Neutrino physics

From collider jets
to neutrino interactions.

Pretraining transfers from high-energy collider jets to few-GeV neutrino–nucleus scattering, across different detectors and underlying physics.

On MINERvA tasks, pretrained models outperform similarly sized models trained from scratch at the same compute budget. OmniLearned shows its strongest gains in available hadronic-energy regression.

04

Knowledge distillation / Real-time inference

Large-model knowledge.
A smaller trigger footprint.

Foundation-model knowledge can be transferred to compact, attention-free Deep Sets students, opening a route toward hardware-trigger deployment.

10³–10⁴×fewer multiply–accumulates at median jet multiplicity

Distillation improves background rejection, particularly at low signal efficiency. Eight-bit quantization-aware training retains nearly all of the floating-point classification accuracy.

Toward trigger deployment: FPGA synthesis, resource estimates, and latency optimization remain work in progress in this study.
